# Experiment 001

## Name

From intent to transaction

## Hypothesis

Can AI transform a user's purchase intent into a real transaction?

## Why this experiment exists

Many AI systems can recommend products.

The question:

Can an AI understand a user's need, constraints and budget well enough to move from recommendation to execution?

## Method

Users provide:

- what they want;
- budget;
- country/location;
- timeline;
- payment preferences.

NULERIS collects and analyzes demand signals.
