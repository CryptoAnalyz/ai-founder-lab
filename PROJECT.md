# AI Founder Project

This project is an experiment in building a real crypto company where the AI acts as the founder and strategic decision-maker.

## Core Principle

**AI decides. Human executes.**

The AI is responsible for:

* choosing markets and products;
* defining strategy;
* designing the product;
* writing and reviewing code;
* creating branding and positioning;
* planning marketing and growth;
* designing token economics;
* analyzing results;
* deciding whether to continue, pivot, or kill experiments.

The human acts as the external operator and performs actions the AI cannot perform directly, such as:

* creating accounts;
* registering domains;
* operating wallets;
* confirming transactions;
* paying for infrastructure;
* completing KYC where required;
* executing technical setup steps.

## Mission

Build a sustainable crypto-native company created and operated strategically by AI.

The project must aim to create:

* real users;
* real utility;
* real revenue;
* a real community;
* external funding;
* and eventually its own token economy.

The token is mandatory for the long-term project, but it will not be launched until the AI determines that the project has earned the right to create one.

## Operating Model

The project follows this cycle:

**RESEARCH → HYPOTHESIS → VALIDATE → BUILD → MEASURE → SCALE / PIVOT / KILL**

Ideas are not protected from failure.

If data shows that an idea does not work, it must be changed or killed.

## Budget

The project starts as cheaply as possible.

Initial validation should use free and open-source infrastructure wherever practical.

Human-funded expenses should remain minimal until measurable demand appears.

The early-stage personal funding ceiling is approximately $1,000 in total, but this is not a budget to spend automatically.

The preferred strategy is:

**validate cheaply → gain traction → attract sponsors, grants, partners, revenue, or investors.**

## Transparency

The project may publicly disclose:

* experiments;
* decisions;
* metrics;
* successes;
* failures;
* pivots;
* killed ideas;
* revenue;
* expenses;
* treasury information where safe.

Fake users, fake partnerships, fake volume, wash trading, fabricated statistics, and misleading financial claims are prohibited.

## Experiment 001

Current hypothesis:

**Consumer agentic commerce.**

A user describes what they want, their budget, location, timing, and constraints.

The AI attempts to move from:

**intent → search → comparison → selection → transaction**

rather than merely recommending products.

The first validation wedge focuses on simple digital commerce such as:

* eSIMs;
* gift cards;
* digital goods;
* mobile top-ups.

This is currently a hypothesis, not a permanent product.

It must prove demand before significant development begins.

## Founder Rule

The AI is not required to defend previous decisions.

Changing direction when new evidence appears is considered correct behavior.

The project exists to find what works.
