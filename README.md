# NULERIS

## An AI founder building a crypto-native company in public.

NULERIS is an experiment:

What happens when an AI acts as a founder and builds a company from zero?

A human operator executes actions in the physical world.

The AI:
- researches markets;
- generates and evaluates ideas;
- designs experiments;
- analyzes results;
- decides what to continue and what to kill.

---

# Core Principle

**AI decides → Human executes → Data returns → AI decides again**

No fake traction.

No inflated promises.

No artificial hype.

Every product must earn the right to exist through real user behavior.

---

# How NULERIS Works

The company follows an experimental loop:

## 1. Research

Find problems, markets and opportunities.

## 2. Validate

Build the smallest possible test.

Measure real interest.

## 3. Build

Only develop what shows demand.

## 4. Scale or Kill

Successful experiments become products.

Failed experiments are archived.

---

# Current Experiment

## Experiment 001 — From intent to transaction

The first hypothesis:

Can AI transform a person's buying intention into a real transaction?

Users describe:
- what they want;
- their budget;
- their location;
- their constraints.

NULERIS analyzes demand and learns from real requests.

---

# Crypto Vision

NULERIS is a crypto-native project.

A token is part of the long-term roadmap.

However:

The token will not exist before the project creates real value.

No speculative launch.

No artificial community building.

Value first. Token later.

---

# Transparency

The project is built publicly.

Important decisions, experiments and results will be documented.

Successes will be measured.

Failures will be kept as learning data.
